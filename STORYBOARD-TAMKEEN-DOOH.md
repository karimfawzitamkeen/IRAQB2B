**Master: 1080 × 1920 · portrait 9:16 · 30 fps · 750 frames · H.264 yuv420p**, the same master as

## 0. Inputs & decisions made autonomously

- **Reference photographs and logo:** supplied by TAMKEEN during production and integrated:
  - **Scene 3** is built on the real *Baghdad International Fair* night photo. The hero screen pulls
    back and lands exactly on the big totem's screen (measured quad). Our animated ads then **replace

  - **Scene 6** is a montage of four real TAMKEEN screens (the giant LED tower, the roadside

  - **Scene 8** uses the **real TAMKEEN logo**, rebuilt as vector art from the supplied file. The hexagon
    cluster was measured hexagon by hexagon and assembles from the converging light. It's the standard

  - The website **www.tamkeen-tech.com** (from the supplied TAMKEEN artwork) joins the contact line.
  - Not used: the fair poster carrying third-party sponsor logos, and the earlier promo artwork

    non-inflated advantages: *مواقع مميزة* · *شاشات عالية الوضوح* · *محتوى رقمي متحرك*.

## 1. Outdoor legibility & safety rules

- **Critical text zone:** x 100–980, y 180–1580.
- **One message at a time**, each on screen for at least about 1 s, except the category words in
  Scene 5, which are an intentional rhythmic burst ending on a held statement.
- **Type:** Cairo Black / ExtraBold for Arabic (130–300 px statements), Inter Black for Latin.
  No text under about 48 px anywhere essential.
- **Contrast:** white or turquoise type on near-black. When type sits over busy content, a dark
  field sits behind it. Every statement also carries a soft glow so it holds up on LED in daylight.
- **Arabic:** RTL; animated by whole word or whole line only (never per letter); no letter-spacing.
- **Flash safety (public LED):** no large-area full-screen luminance flashes faster than 3 per
  second (WCAG general-flash guidance). The energy comes from **motion** (pushes, slams, slices,
  tunnels, streaks). Rapid category words change text and accent only; the background stays dark.
  Flashes are single, brief, partial-area accents.

## 4. Scenes

**1 · Attention (0–75).** Black. A single turquoise point with an electronic pulse ring. It snaps open
into the outline of a portrait LED screen (corners draw, brief edge flash). Camera push. **"تُرى"**
slams in at about 300 px, lit from within the screen (scan-lines pass through the glyphs). Then
**"اجعل علامتك"** lands above it, completing *اجعل علامتك تُرى*. A partial pulse hands over to the power-on.

**2 · The screen comes alive (75–150).** The screen powers on and rises to the upper frame. Five
generated ads cycle, each with a different transition: fashion (vertical wipe), technology
(rotating slices), services (split frame), retail (pixel reconstruction), exhibitions (glass
distortion band). Lower band: **"إعلانك هنا"** *slams* in (scale 2→1 with motion blur), then pushes
out upward as **"في قلب الحدث"** *rises* word by word under a turquoise underline sweep.

**3 · Real-world impact (150–240).** The camera pulls back fast. The screen is a **totem standing in
a night plaza**: skyline silhouettes, lamp bokeh, a reflective wet ground carrying the screen's light
spill, and dark foreground crowd silhouettes with parallax. One screen becomes **two** (slides in
from depth), then **three**, then a whole row of screens, each playing different content. Top band:
**"حضور أقوى"**, then **"مشاهدة أكثر"** (never together).

**4 · The network (240–330).** The plaza dims and the screens become **luminous nodes** on a dark
perspective map grid. Turquoise data lines connect them, with cyan pulses running along. The camera
**flies through** a screen tunnel, with screens passing on both sides, each showing an ad, while the
speed ramps up. **"شبكة إعلانية رقمية"**, then **"تعمل من أجلك"**, which physically flies from deep
space to the viewer, holds, then rushes past the camera into Scene 5.

**5 · Your brand owns the screen (330–435).** A blank billboard rotates toward the camera. A generic
brand assembles on it: LOGO placeholder, headline, product shape, CTA pill. The billboard **bursts**
into six creative variations flying outward. Rapid rhythm (5 frames each): **مطاعم · أزياء · تقنية ·
خدمات · معارض · تجارة**. Then **"مكانك هنا"**, huge and held.

**6 · High visibility (435–525).** A montage of four generated outdoor locations (exhibition hall,
avenue of screens, plaza crowd, gateway). Transitions: vertical slices, perspective panels,
zoom-through and light-streak wipes. **"علامتك أمام الجمهور"** transforms into **"كل يوم"**,
extremely large.

**7 · TAMKEEN advantage (525–615).** Everything accelerates: a hyper-tunnel of screens and light
streaks, with the hexagon grid pulsing. Three giant statements, one at a time: **مواقع مميزة** ·
**شاشات عالية الوضوح** · **محتوى رقمي متحرك**.

**8 · Brand recall (615–750).** All screens and lines converge and assemble the **TAMKEEN logo**
(hexagon cluster, centre first, then outward). **TAMKEEN** wordmark (thin, turquoise).
**"إعلانك في قلب الحدث"**. **Contact us : 07803158768** (number at about 116 px), then **www.tamkeen-tech.com**. Everything is settled by
about 690f, held for 1.7 s, then fades out at 742–750.

## 5. Motion language

Fast camera pushes and pull-backs · speed ramps (expo in/out) · slams with overshoot · velocity
motion blur and light trails · perspective screens with reflections · slices, masks and pixel
reconstruction for ad changes · data lines with running pulses · hexagon motif as the connective
geometry · restrained glitch accents (a two-frame RGB offset on hits) · the camera never sits still.
