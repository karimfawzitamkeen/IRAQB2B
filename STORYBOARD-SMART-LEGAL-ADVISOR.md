## 1. Creative concept — "من التعقيد القانوني إلى الوضوح الذكي"

**One continuous vertical journey, following one object.** A legal **document** is born out of
chaos and never leaves the screen. It becomes:

```
fragments → structured document → (portal) → opened law text → contract → bilingual contract
→ legal question → consultation response → case file → secure core → gold arch motif → brand
```

Each scene hands this object to the next. The emotional arc is
**Complexity → Knowledge → Analysis → Professional Output → One Integrated Platform**, and it's
mirrored in the light: scattered and dim at first, then ordered, then luminous and calm.

**Recurring motif, the arch (القوس):** a thin gold pointed arch, inspired by Middle Eastern
architecture and read as a gateway or portal. It first appears as the **portal** in Scene 2 and
returns as the **brand emblem** in Scene 8. It stands for "بوابة" (gateway) without cliché court
imagery: no gavels, no scales, no columns.

- Arabic text is **centred on x 540** or **right-aligned to x 900** (RTL), never left-aligned.

## 5. Motion language & transitions

**Easing**
- `expoOut`: arrivals and reveals
- `expoInOut`: camera moves and document travel
- `backOut`: small snaps (clause rows locking in, access gates closing)
- Linear only for continuous systems (orbits, sweeps, drift)

**Rules**
1. **Vertical first.** The primary movement runs on the Y axis: the document rises into the portal,
   contract rows assemble upward, the question drops into the core, the scan sweeps down and
   streams converge. The camera cranes and pushes rather than panning sideways.
2. **One object, never lost.** Every scene hands the document or its current form to the next. No
   hard cuts and no standalone cross-dissolves.
3. **Draw-on before fill.** Frames and outlines draw in thin gold line, then glass surfaces fill in.
4. **Restraint.** Only one element animates prominently at a time. Supporting motion stays subtle.
   Particles are sparse and slow. No neon and no glitch effects.
5. **Depth.** Three parallax planes (far atmosphere, mid hero, near defocused fragments). Motion blur
   applies only to fast travel (velocity-proportional, restrained).
6. **Honest representation.** Highlights sit *behind* legal text, explanations sit *beside* it, and
   AI outputs are framed as organised assistance.

**Transitions**
| Handoff | Frames | Move |
|---------|--------|------|
| 1 → 2 | 80–112 | Fragments form a document, which rises into the gold arch portal |
| 2 → 3 | 166–186 | Library node detaches and the camera pushes through it into the library space |
| 3 → 4 | 246–262 | The opened law page folds and re-opens as a contract |
| 4 (internal) | 298–310 | The contract splits into Arabic and English columns |
| 4 → 5 | 336–352 | The columns collapse into a line, which becomes the question card |
| 5 → 6 | 428–442 | The response card files itself into a case folder |
| 6 → 7 | 530–548 | The case file and outputs stream as light into the secure core |
| 7 → 8 | 618–664 | Core and pillars converge, collapse and draw the gold arch emblem |

## 6. Typography

| Role | Font | Size / weight | Notes |
|------|------|---------------|-------|
| Arabic statements (S1) | IBM Plex Sans Arabic | 84–96 px / 300 | centred, ≤ 2 lines |
| Arabic brand name (S2, S8) | IBM Plex Sans Arabic | 88–96 px / 600 | 2 lines: منصة المستشار / القانوني الذكي |
| Arabic service titles (top band) | IBM Plex Sans Arabic | 80 px / 600 | one line, centred |
| Arabic supporting lines | IBM Plex Sans Arabic | 34–44 px / 400 | gold dot separators |
| Arabic UI labels / chips | IBM Plex Sans Arabic | 28–32 px / 500 | |
| Legal text texture (fragments, law page, contract) | Amiri (Naskh) | scales with the object | the "voice of the law", used on documents only |
| English brand line | Inter | 32 px / 500 | uppercase, tracked 10 px |
| English translation column | Inter | 26–30 px / 400 | |
| Instrumentation (hash glyphs, tiny indices) | JetBrains Mono | 20–24 px | subtle, never essential |

**Arabic typesetting rules (non-negotiable)**
- `direction: rtl`, with correct bidi isolation for any mixed Arabic, English or number runs.
- **No letter-spacing and no per-letter animation on Arabic.** Both break cursive joining.
  Arabic animates **by word or by line** (mask wipes run right-to-left, with blur-to-sharp).
- No stretching or synthetic distortion. Natural font shaping only (no tatweel/kashida insertion).
- Arabic-Indic digits (٠١٢…) inside Arabic legal fragments. Western digits only in English or mono.
- Arabic line-height 1.3–1.4, so diacritics and descenders never collide.

**Hierarchy:** one statement per moment. The order of attention is always **service name →
visual → supporting line**. Every scene is readable in under one second on a phone.

## 8. Content guardrails

- No claims of guaranteed legal success or court outcomes, of replacing licensed lawyers, of
  automatic government approval, or of official judicial authority.
- No capabilities beyond the six named services. No invented statistics, law names or real case numbers.
  All legal fragments are generic.
- AI is shown as **assistance and organisation** (analysis, memo, follow-up), never as a decision-maker.
- No gavels, scales, cartoon lawyers, AI avatars, glowing brains, padlocks, stock footage or neon cyberpunk.
